# Namma Sahaaya AI
![alt text](image.png)
## Voice\-Powered Kannada Digital Literacy Assistant

### Empowering Kannada Speakers to Understand the Digital World

---

## Problem Statement

Millions of Kannada\-speaking users receive banking alerts, government notifications, UPI messages, OTPs, and smartphone\-related instructions every day. Many users, especially senior citizens, farmers, rural communities, and first\-time smartphone users, struggle to understand these digital communications due to language and technology barriers.

This often leads to confusion, reduced digital adoption, and vulnerability to misinformation and fraud.

---

## Solution

**Namma Sahaaya AI** is a voice\-powered digital literacy assistant designed to help Kannada speakers interact with technology confidently.

The system listens to a user's voice query in Kannada, converts the speech into text, generates a simple and understandable explanation, and responds back through natural voice output.

This enables users to understand digital services without needing advanced technical knowledge.

---

## Key Features

### 🎤 Kannada Speech\-to\-Text

Converts spoken Kannada queries into accurate text using **Prisma STT**.

### 🤖 Intelligent Query Understanding

Processes user questions and provides simple Kannada explanations.

### 🔊 Kannada Text\-to\-Speech

Generates natural voice responses using **Timbre TTS**.

### 📱 Digital Literacy Support

Helps users understand:

- UPI Payments
- KYC Verification
- Banking Alerts
- OTP Messages
- Smartphone Features
- Basic Digital Services

### 📩 SMS Explainer

Explains commonly received digital and banking messages in simple Kannada.

## Example Use Cases

### Use Case 1

**User Query:**

UPI PIN ಅಂದರೆ ಏನು?

**Response:**

UPI PIN ನಿಮ್ಮ ಡಿಜಿಟಲ್ ಪಾವತಿಗಳನ್ನು ಸುರಕ್ಷಿತಗೊಳಿಸುವ ರಹಸ್ಯ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ.
